Total sales for fiscal year 2023 were $36.7 billion, an increase compared to the prior fiscal year, reflecting growth across most of the company's reportable segments including Aerospace, Building Automation, and Energy and Sustainability Solutions. Organic sales growth was driven by strong commercial aviation demand, continued recovery in build rates, and healthy order backlog across several product lines. Currency translation had a modest favorable effect on reported sales growth for the year.

Aerospace segment margin was negatively impacted by higher material costs stemming from persistent input cost inflation across the segment's supply chain during fiscal year 2023. Pricing actions partially offset the impact of inflation, though the net effect on segment margin remained unfavorable compared to the prior fiscal year. Higher commercial aftermarket volume and continued growth in commercial original equipment shipments provided some offsetting benefit to overall segment profitability during the year.

Building Automation segment sales grew for the fiscal year, supported by continued demand for building management systems, fire and security products, and energy efficiency solutions across commercial and institutional customers. Industrial Automation segment sales were roughly flat, as strength in warehouse and workflow solutions was offset by softer demand in productivity solutions and sensing technologies. Both segments continued to benefit from long-term secular trends toward automation and building efficiency investment.

Management issued fiscal 2024 guidance calling for continued sales growth across most segments, supported by a healthy order backlog and continued recovery in commercial aerospace build rates. The company expects margin expansion in the coming fiscal year as pricing actions and productivity initiatives continue to more than offset residual input cost inflation. Capital allocation priorities remain focused on organic investment, disciplined acquisitions, and continued capital returns to shareholders.
