Total sales and revenues for fiscal year 2023 were $67.1 billion, an increase of 13% compared to the prior year, reflecting broad-based strength across the company's three primary operating segments. Growth was driven by higher sales volume, favorable price realization, and continued strong demand for construction, mining, and power generation equipment across most regions the company serves. Services revenue also grew steadily, supported by an expanding population of equipment in the field requiring parts and maintenance support.

Sales volume for machines increased across Construction Industries and Resource Industries during fiscal year 2023, supported by healthy end-user demand and continued replenishment of dealer inventories following prior-year constraints. The increase was due to higher sales volume in most regions, combined with favorable price realization that further contributed to segment revenue growth for the year. Construction Industries benefited from robust non-residential construction activity, while Resource Industries saw continued strength in mining equipment demand driven by commodity investment cycles.

Energy and Transportation segment sales and revenues grew for the fiscal year, driven by strong demand for reciprocating engines used in oil and gas applications, power generation, and marine propulsion. The segment also benefited from continued strength in locomotive services and solar turbine sales, supported by global infrastructure investment and elevated energy sector capital spending. Backlog for the segment remained elevated entering the next fiscal year, reflecting sustained customer demand across most end markets served.

Operating profit margin expanded for fiscal year 2023, reflecting the combined benefit of higher sales volume, favorable price realization, and continued manufacturing cost discipline across the company's operating segments. Management expects continued, albeit more moderate, sales and revenue growth in the coming fiscal year, supported by a healthy backlog and resilient demand across construction, resource, and energy end markets. The company reiterated its commitment to returning capital to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases.
