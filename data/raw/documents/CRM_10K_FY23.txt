Total revenues were $31.35 billion, an increase of 18% year over year, reflecting continued demand for Salesforce's Customer 360 platform across sales, service, marketing, and commerce clouds. Growth was broad-based across geographies, with particular strength in large enterprise accounts adopting multiple clouds simultaneously through expanded and renewed contracts. Currency headwinds had a modest negative effect on as-reported growth, while constant-currency revenue growth remained similarly strong across every major product line, industry vertical, and region served by the company.

Subscription and support revenues were $29.02 billion, an increase of 18%, representing the substantial majority of total revenue and reflecting strong renewal rates alongside continued new customer additions across every cloud. Professional services and other revenues were $2.33 billion, an increase of 27%, as customers increasingly engaged the company's services organization to accelerate platform adoption, integration, and implementation timelines following new contract signings. Both revenue streams grew faster than the prior fiscal year, with professional services expanding from a comparatively smaller revenue base.

Operating margin improved meaningfully year over year as the company maintained disciplined expense management across sales, marketing, and general and administrative functions throughout the fiscal year. Non-GAAP operating margin expanded, benefiting from a restructuring plan announced earlier in the year that reduced headcount and consolidated the company's real estate footprint. Management emphasized a continued focus on profitable growth rather than growth at any cost, a deliberate shift in strategic priorities communicated clearly to investors.

Remaining performance obligation, representing total future contracted revenue not yet recognized, grew at a double-digit rate year over year, reflecting continued momentum in multi-year enterprise agreements signed during the fiscal year. Management issued fiscal 2024 revenue guidance calling for continued growth, albeit at a more moderate pace than the prior year, citing a cautious macroeconomic environment and lengthening enterprise sales cycles. The company reiterated its commitment to expanding operating margin further in the coming fiscal year.
