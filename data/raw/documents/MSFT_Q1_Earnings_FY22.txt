Revenue for the first quarter of fiscal year 2022, ended September 30, 2021, was $45.3 billion, an increase of 22% compared to the prior-year quarter, reflecting broad-based demand across the company's cloud, productivity, and gaming businesses. Growth during the quarter was driven by continued strength in commercial cloud offerings alongside a rebound in personal computing device demand following pandemic-related disruptions earlier in the prior year. Operating income for the quarter also grew at a double-digit rate compared to the prior-year quarter.

Intelligent Cloud revenue for the quarter was $17.0 billion, driven by continued enterprise migration to cloud infrastructure and platform services during the period. Azure and other cloud services revenue grew 50% in the quarter, reflecting sustained demand for hybrid cloud, artificial intelligence, and data analytics workloads among enterprise customers. Server products and cloud services revenue growth for the quarter was supported by continued growth in Azure consumption among both large enterprise and small business customers.

During the quarter, the company returned capital to shareholders through dividend payments and share repurchases, reflecting continued confidence in the business heading into the remainder of fiscal year 2022. The board of directors declared a quarterly dividend payable in the following quarter, consistent with the company's established capital return program. Management reiterated its intention to continue balancing capital returns with ongoing investment in cloud infrastructure and datacenter capacity expansion during the current fiscal year.
