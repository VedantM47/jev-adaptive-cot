Fourth quarter revenue was $5.05 billion, representing growth compared to the prior-year quarter and reflecting continued momentum across the company's Digital Media and Digital Experience businesses heading into fiscal 2024. Subscription revenue represented the substantial majority of total quarterly revenue, underscoring the durability of the company's recurring revenue model. Results for the quarter exceeded the high end of previously issued management guidance across nearly every key financial metric.

Digital Experience segment operating margin decreased due to higher sales and marketing investments made during the fourth quarter to expand go-to-market capacity ahead of fiscal 2024. The company increased headcount within its enterprise sales organization and expanded marketing programs supporting Adobe Experience Cloud, weighing on near-term segment profitability. Management characterized the investment as deliberate and expects the additional sales and marketing capacity to support accelerated Digital Experience growth in future quarters.

Digital Media net new annualized recurring revenue for the fourth quarter reflected continued healthy subscriber growth across Creative Cloud and Document Cloud offerings during the period. Growth was supported by strong demand among individual creators alongside expanding adoption among enterprise customers migrating additional seats to cloud-based subscription plans. The company highlighted continued strong engagement with generative artificial intelligence features as a meaningful contributor to net new subscriber additions during the quarter.

Management introduced fiscal 2024 targets calling for continued revenue growth across both Digital Media and Digital Experience segments, supported by ongoing generative artificial intelligence product introductions throughout the year. The targets reflect an expectation of continued healthy subscriber growth alongside a gradual improvement in overall company profitability as recent sales and marketing investments begin to yield returns. Management expressed confidence in the company's competitive position heading into the new fiscal year.
