Total revenue for fiscal year 2023 was $19.41 billion, an increase of 10% compared to the prior fiscal year, reflecting continued strength across the company's core creative, document, and experience businesses. Growth was driven by sustained subscriber additions, price increases implemented across select product tiers, and steady expansion of the company's generative artificial intelligence features embedded throughout its flagship applications. Currency movements had a modest unfavorable effect on reported revenue growth for the year.

Digital Media segment operating income was driven by higher subscription revenue, as continued growth in Creative Cloud subscriptions and steady average revenue per user more than offset increased spending on research and development and go-to-market activities. The segment benefited from strong retention among existing subscribers alongside healthy growth in new subscriber additions across both individual and enterprise plans. Management noted that generative AI features embedded within Creative Cloud applications contributed meaningfully to subscriber engagement and renewal rates during the fiscal year.

Digital Experience revenue grew at a healthy double-digit rate for the fiscal year, driven by expanding customer adoption of Adobe Experience Cloud across marketing, analytics, and commerce use cases among large enterprise customers. Subscription revenue within Digital Experience outpaced the segment's overall growth rate, reflecting a continued shift toward recurring cloud-based offerings rather than perpetual licenses and professional services engagements. New customer wins and expanded deployments among existing customers both contributed to the segment's revenue growth during the year.

Research and development investment increased year over year, with a growing share of spending directed toward generative artificial intelligence capabilities across the company's creative, document, and experience product lines. The company continued to expand its Firefly family of generative AI models, integrating new capabilities into flagship applications while also investing in infrastructure to support future AI-driven product innovation. Management described artificial intelligence as the company's top strategic investment priority heading into the next fiscal year.
