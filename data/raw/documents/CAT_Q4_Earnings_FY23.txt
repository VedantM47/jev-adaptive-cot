Results for the quarter were consistent with management's expectations communicated earlier in the fiscal year.

Management noted no material discrepancy between quarterly and annual machine sales volume trends discussed on the earnings call.

Full-year 2023 profit per share increased compared to the prior fiscal year, reflecting higher sales and revenues alongside continued operating margin expansion across the company's primary segments. Management highlighted disciplined capital allocation during the year, including continued share repurchases and dividend increases, as contributing to per-share profit growth.
